
# Preschool Admission in Delhi: A Parent's Checklist Before Enrolling Your Child

Starting preschool is a major milestone for children and their families. Parents need to consider several factors before selecting a school, including age eligibility, teaching methods, daily schedules, safety and admission requirements.

This checklist can help families researching a playschool in Delhi prepare for the admission process.

## 1. Understand Your Child's Readiness

Children develop at different rates. Consider whether your child is beginning to communicate basic needs, explore new activities and become comfortable with familiar adults.

Children do not need to master academic skills before joining preschool.

## 2. Check Age Eligibility

Preschools may offer playgroup, pre-nursery, nursery and kindergarten programmes.

Confirm the age criteria for each programme directly with the school because eligibility requirements can differ.

## 3. Visit the School

A school visit allows parents to observe classroom activities, teacher interactions, cleanliness and the overall learning environment.

Try to understand what an ordinary school day looks like.

## 4. Review the Fee Structure

Ask for a written breakdown of registration fees, tuition, uniforms, learning materials, meals, transport and daycare charges, if applicable.

Understand payment schedules and refund policies before completing admission.

## 5. Confirm Required Documents

Schools may request documents such as:

- Child's birth certificate.
- Recent photographs.
- Parent identification.
- Residential address details.
- Medical information, where required.

Always confirm the official document checklist with the school.

## 6. Understand Timings and Daycare

Working parents should confirm class timings, extended care options, pick-up procedures and additional charges.

Check whether the available schedule suits your family's daily routine.

## 7. Discuss the Child's Settling-In Process

Ask how teachers support children who feel anxious or take time to adjust.

A gradual and reassuring transition can make the first school experience more comfortable.

## 8. Review Admission Information

Confirm programme availability, admission eligibility, fees and required documents directly with the respective centre.

## Conclusion

A well-planned admission decision begins with understanding your child's needs and reviewing the school's everyday practices. Use this checklist to compare options, visit shortlisted schools and clarify important details before enrolling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. When should parents start searching for preschool admission?

It is useful to begin researching a few months before the preferred joining date so families have time to compare schools and complete the admission process.

### 2. What documents are needed for preschool admission?

Commonly requested documents may include a birth certificate, photographs and parent identification. Requirements vary by school.

### 3. Does a child need to know letters and numbers before preschool?

No. Preschool provides opportunities to develop early language, number awareness, communication and other foundational skills.

### 4. Should parents choose a preschool with daycare?

Families should consider their schedules and childcare needs. Confirm timings, supervision, activities and additional charges before enrolling.

### 5. How can parents prepare children for their first day?

Introduce a predictable morning routine, talk positively about school and give children time to become familiar with the new environment.
